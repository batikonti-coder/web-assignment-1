Midterm Project: Student Task Manager

Project Description

My midterm project is a Travel Website designed for people who love to explore new countries. The target users are tourists, families, and adventure seekers looking for vacation packages. The main purpose of the website is to provide information about popular destinations, offer easy tour bookings, and share travel guides to help users plan their perfect trip.

Sitemap

My website will consist of the following 5 pages:

  • 1. Home: Main page with a search bar for flights and top destination highlights.
  • 2. Destinations: A catalog of countries and cities with descriptions.
  • 3. Tours: Detailed tour packages, pricing, and itineraries.
  • 4. About Us: Information about our travel agency, team, and reviews.
  • 5. Contact: A booking form and our agency's contact information.

Wireframes

I created the wireframes using Figma. Each page includes a consistent header (Logo + Navigation), main content area, and a footer (Copyright + Links).
