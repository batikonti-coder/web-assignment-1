Luis Suárez

1987 - Present | "El Pistolero"

About Luis Suárez

Luis Alberto Suárez Díaz is a Uruguayan professional footballer born in 1987 in Salto, Uruguay. Known for his incredible finishing, passing, and fierce competitiveness, he is widely regarded as one of the best strikers of his generation.

Career Timeline

  1. 1987: Born in Salto, Uruguay.
  2. 2007: Moved to Europe to join Ajax.
  3. 2011: Transferred to Liverpool.
  4. 2014: Joined FC Barcelona.
  5. 2020: Signed with Atlético Madrid.
  6. 2024: Reunited with Lionel Messi at Inter Miami.
